# Orgainse Spatial UI Redesign: Snapshot First

The whole Orgainse website is redesigned in a "spatial" style. Real 3D shapes and scenes rotate and react as visitors scroll or move the mouse, using the new 9-colour palette and 8-role type system.
The first step is a set of picture mockups, so the look can be approved before any page of the live site changes.

## Who it's for
- **You (the Orgainse owner)**: to judge the new look before committing to a full rebuild.
- **Later, every visitor and admin user**: once the look is approved and rolled out.

## Core features and experience
- **Snapshot (built now)**: still picture mockups shown in chat. Each shows the 3D scene as one frozen frame, with a note on how it moves. It covers:
  - **Home**: a 3D hero scene, then the services, ORQYNE, frequently asked questions and footer sections. Shown on desktop and mobile.
  - **Services page**: its own 3D scene, with a service popup open on top.
  - **Products (ORQYNE) page**: a 3D scene that suggests the product, such as linked nodes for portfolios, risks and decisions.
  - **Careers page**: role cards, with a job popup open.
  - **Contact and booking**: the "Book Consultation" popup.
  - **Admin Dashboard**: overview, plus the blog or careers editor, in the dark style.
  - **Style sheet**: the palette, type roles, 3D shape family, buttons, inputs and cards.
- **What "spatial" means here**:
  - **Shapes**: real 3D objects drawn in the browser, built from the logo's language: spheres, rings, orbits and soft geometric forms.
  - **Movement**: shapes rotate slowly on their own. They tilt and parallax toward the mouse on desktop, and shift and re-arrange as the page scrolls.
  - **Lighting and finish**: soft studio lighting, with surfaces ranging from matte to softly glossy. Colour comes from the palette.
  - **Content**: text, buttons and cards stay clear, flat and readable in front of the 3D scenes. The 3D sits behind and between the content and never hides it.
  - **Variety**: each main page gets its own 3D scene and lead colour, so every page feels distinct but belongs to one family.
- **Two moods**:
  - **Public pages and popups**: light and airy, with bright backgrounds and coloured 3D shapes.
  - **Admin**: dark and immersive, with deep navy space and glowing 3D accents. The 3D is kept to headers and empty states so daily work stays fast and uncluttered.
  - **Shared accents**: the dark mood also appears in a few places on the public site, such as the footer and one feature band, so the two feel related.
- **Palette roles**:
  - **Electric blue #0062FD**: main actions and links.
  - **Sky #0BD5FD**: highlights and glows.
  - **Navy #00007D**: headings, dark surfaces and admin backgrounds.
  - **Black #000000 and white #FFFFFF**: text and backgrounds.
  - **Cream #F7E7BC**: warm backgrounds.
  - **Teal #39B29A**: success and progress.
  - **Coral #E07A5F**: secondary accents.
  - **Amber #FFC300**: badges, hiring stages and small emphasis.
- **Type roles**: shown with free lookalikes for now. The real licensed fonts drop in later without changing the design.

## User flow
1. You review the snapshot pictures in chat.
2. You approve the look or ask for changes, such as the 3D shapes, colour balance, amount of motion, fonts or the admin mood. The snapshot is updated until it is right.
3. On approval, the full rollout starts (phases 2 and 3). Until then, nothing on the live site changes.

## UI/UX feel
Bold, modern and premium, like a design-led technology company. The 3D scenes give each page a sense of space and movement, while plenty of whitespace and calm content keep it executive-friendly. Colour is used with restraint: electric blue and sky lead, while cream, teal, coral and amber add warmth and meaning. Motion is smooth and slow rather than busy, and text always has strong contrast.

## Implementation phases
- **Phase 1 (MVP, built now): snapshot.** Picture mockups of the screens listed above, plus the style sheet. The live site is untouched.
- **Phase 2: public site rollout.**
  - **Coverage**: every public page, subpage and popup is rebuilt in the approved style, with live interactive 3D scenes. This includes Home, Services, Products, About, Contact, Careers with role popups, Blog, Newsletter, the assessment, the calculator, booking, Privacy, Terms, the cookie banner, the 404 page and the unsubscribe page.
  - **Kept as they are**: all current content, search optimisation, page structure and share links.
  - **Share cards**: re-themed to the new palette.
- **Phase 3: admin and the rest.**
  - **Admin**: the full Admin Dashboard, login, every editor and every manager, in the dark immersive style.
  - **Emails**: newsletter and notification emails restyled to match within email limits. Emails can't show 3D, so they use still images of the 3D shapes.
  - **Real fonts**: the licensed fonts are swapped in once you provide them.

## Assumptions
- "Spatial UI" means interactive 3D scenes that rotate and react to scrolling and the mouse. It is not a glass or Apple Vision Pro style, and it is not VR or headset content.
- The 3D shapes are abstract, built from the logo's circles, rings and orbits. There are no people, characters or mascots, in line with your earlier share-card feedback.
- Your earlier mood answers are combined: light for the public site, dark immersive for the admin, and dark accent bands on the public site so the two connect.
- **Speed and devices**:
  - **Phones and older devices**: lighter scenes, with a still image where 3D would be slow.
  - **Reduced motion**: visitors whose device asks for less motion get still versions.
  - **Search results**: Google speed and search visibility must not drop.
- The new palette replaces the current one everywhere. The logo itself stays unchanged.
- Mockups are polished concept images. Exact spacing and motion details are finalised during the rollout.
- All current wording stays as it is; only the look changes. Mockups use the site's real text where it fits.
- The Neue Machina, Gordita, Univers, Panton, Nexa, Gabriel Sans and Hero licences are your responsibility for the live site. Lookalikes stay in place until the licensed files are supplied.
- Accessibility (readable contrast, clear focus states, keyboard use) is kept to at least today's level.
