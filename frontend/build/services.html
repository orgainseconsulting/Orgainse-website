Frequently Asked Questions

Scope, engagement model, boundaries, and how pricing is determined.

1
Scope and Engagement

What does Orgainse actually do?

Orgainse helps organisations improve project delivery, workflow visibility, operational governance, and practical Artificial Intelligence adoption. Services include Project Management as a Service, Artificial Intelligence and workflow transformation, delivery and operational governance, ORQYNE implementation and adoption, Artificial Intelligence strategy and readiness, and separately scoped Healthcare Revenue Intelligence Advisory.

What is Project Management as a Service?

Project Management as a Service (PMaaS) gives an organisation access to structured project leadership, governance, reporting, risk management, stakeholder communication, and delivery support without immediately building a full internal Project Management Office. It can be scoped to one critical project or to a delivery portfolio.

How is Orgainse different from a conventional consulting firm?

Orgainse combines advisory, implementation, operating support, and ORQYNE. The engagement is designed to move from recommendation to an operating workflow with defined owners, controls, reporting, and adoption support, rather than ending at a deck.

Who does Orgainse work with?

Primarily Information Technology services firms, software delivery teams, startups, small and medium-sized enterprises, and cross-functional operating teams. Typical buyers are founders, Chief Executive Officers, Chief Operating Officers, delivery leaders, project leaders, operations heads, and business owners.

2
How Engagements Run

How does an engagement start?

It starts with a focused discovery session on one important execution problem. We clarify the desired outcome, current workflow, decision owners, operating constraints, and the most practical next step. The recommendation may involve consulting, Project Management as a Service, ORQYNE, a short diagnostic, or no immediate engagement.

What are the four stages of delivery?

Diagnose: clarify the desired outcome, current workflow, decision owners, constraints, risks, and measurable sources of leakage. Design: create the target workflow, governance model, reporting rhythm, roles, controls, and success measures. Implement: configure the process, ORQYNE workspace, templates, integrations, and operating cadence. Govern: review adoption, risks, decisions, delivery health, and measurable outcomes on a defined schedule.

Can Orgainse work with our existing tools?

Yes. Orgainse can improve the operating model around an existing tool such as Jira, Asana or monday.com, introduce ORQYNE for selected workflows, or assess whether consolidation is justified. The recommendation is based on business fit rather than forced replacement, and integration availability is confirmed during scoping.

How is Artificial Intelligence introduced safely?

We map the process first, identify where Artificial Intelligence improves decision quality or removes friction, and define human approval points before anything is built. Artificial Intelligence output may be incomplete or incorrect, so material operational, financial, legal, security, healthcare, or employment decisions retain appropriate human review.

3
Healthcare Revenue Intelligence Advisory

What is Healthcare Revenue Intelligence Advisory?

It is an advisory and analytics service that improves visibility into revenue performance, denial patterns, payer behaviour, operating controls, and executive reporting. It works alongside your existing revenue cycle operations and vendors rather than replacing them. Each engagement is separately scoped for data handling, regulatory considerations, and advisory boundaries.

Who is the right fit for this service?

Organisations that already have revenue cycle operations in place, internally or through a third party, and need an independent view of what those operations are delivering and where performance can be strengthened through better governance, analytics, or technology readiness.

What is explicitly not included?

Orgainse does not provide medical billing or claims submission, medical coding or clinical documentation, accounts receivable follow-up or collections, denial follow-up or appeal submission, payment posting or remittance processing, patient billing, or onshore and offshore revenue cycle staffing. The scope is strictly advisory, analytics, and governance.

4
Pricing, Outcomes and Data

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on scope, engagement duration, the services involved, the operating support required, and — where ORQYNE is included — users, enabled modules, implementation requirements, Artificial Intelligence usage model, and integration scope. Final scope and commercial terms are confirmed in writing before work begins.

Does Orgainse guarantee delivery improvement or Return on Investment?

No. Timing and outcomes depend on scope, data quality, leadership decisions, adoption, integrations, operating conditions, and customer responsibilities. Success measures are agreed before work begins and reviewed through the engagement, and we deliberately do not publish guaranteed percentages.

Are the assessment and calculator on this site an audit?

No. The Artificial Intelligence readiness assessment and the Return on Investment calculator are directional planning aids based entirely on the answers and assumptions you supply. They are not a certification, an audit, or professional advice for your specific circumstances.

How is our data handled?

Data handling, access, retention, and confidentiality are defined in the engagement agreement, and Healthcare engagements are separately scoped for regulatory considerations. Orgainse does not claim certification or legal compliance under any standard unless it has been formally established and independently verified.

Delivery consulting, Project Management as a Service and delivery governance

Orgainse is a delivery-execution partner for Information Technology services firms, software teams, startups and growing businesses. We work on one operating condition: service-led, cross-functional work that needs stronger execution visibility and governance. Engagements combine consulting, Project Management as a Service, workflow transformation and implementation of ORQYNE, our delivery-governance workspace.

The problem we work on is narrow and specific: work is spread across disconnected tools, status is reconstructed manually, ownership is unclear, risks surface late, and decisions are made without a reliable view of delivery health. Our role is to replace that with structured operating rhythms — named owners, tracked risks and actions, agreed reporting cadence and a single view leadership can rely on.

Orgainse was founded in 2025 and operates from Austin, Texas (headquarters) and Bengaluru, India (corporate office). We do not publish guaranteed percentages, savings figures or delivery improvements. Success measures are agreed in writing before work begins and reviewed through the engagement.

How we work

Agreed measures, not promises

Every engagement is scoped around measures both sides agree on up front — delivery health, decision turnaround, forecast reliability — reviewed at an agreed cadence.

Visible, governed execution

Risks, actions, dependencies and owners are tracked in one place, so status is a by-product of the work instead of a weekly reporting exercise.

Practitioners, not observers

Delivery leads and governance practitioners who sit inside the operating rhythm, run the cadence and hand it back to your team as a repeatable standard.
