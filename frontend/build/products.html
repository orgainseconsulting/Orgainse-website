Ground Artificial Intelligence in Your Approved Operating Knowledge

ORQYNE can retrieve relevant information from tenant-approved documents, projects, decisions, work items, and knowledge sources before generating an Artificial Intelligence-assisted response. The objective is to make Artificial Intelligence more context-aware while retaining user permissions, provider configuration, approval controls, and human accountability. Artificial Intelligence-generated output may contain errors. Users remain responsible for reviewing material recommendations and actions.

Index

Records, project files and connected-tool content are indexed by meaning and by exact words, inside your workspace only.

Retrieve

Only records the person is allowed to see are used, and linked records are checked again at every step.

Answer

Answers link to their sources and say when information is missing, out of date or contradictory. Follow-ups keep their context.

Approve

Suggested changes arrive as drafts. Nothing changes until an authorised person approves.

Artificial Intelligence output may be incomplete or incorrect. Material operational, financial, legal, security, healthcare, or employment decisions must retain appropriate human review.

Key terms
RAID log IT Services
A register of Risks, Assumptions, Issues and Dependencies for a project or programme.
Earned value IT Services
A method that compares planned work, completed work and actual cost to measure project performance.
Schedule performance index (SPI) IT Services
Earned value divided by planned value; below 1 means the project is behind schedule.
Sprint scope IT Services
The work, usually in story points, committed to a sprint.
Velocity IT Services
The amount of work a team completes in a sprint, used to plan future sprints.
Change request IT Services
A formal proposal to change a project's scope, schedule or cost.
Delivery governance Both products
The rules, roles and reviews that keep delivery on track, visible and accountable.
Portfolio management Both products
Managing a group of projects and programmes together to meet business goals.
Project management office (PMO) Both products
The team that sets standards and oversees projects across an organisation.
Cost performance index (CPI) IT Services
Earned value divided by actual cost; below 1 means the project is over budget.
Estimate at completion (EAC) IT Services
The forecast total cost of a project when it finishes.
Rate card IT Services
The agreed prices for roles or services, used for time-and-materials billing.
Time and materials IT Services
A contract where the client pays for time spent and materials used.
Verified value Canvas
Value from people's contributions that reviewers have checked and confirmed.
Value Map Canvas
A view of where verified value comes from across the organisation.
Capacity forecast Canvas
A projection of the people-time available against planned work.
Approval routing Canvas
Sending each request to the person with the right decision rights and spending limit.
Spending limit Canvas
The largest amount a role can approve before a request goes higher.
Temporary cover Canvas
A person who takes an approver's decisions for a set period.
Dotted-line reporting Canvas
A secondary reporting relationship alongside a person's main manager.
Organisation health check Canvas
Automatic checks for gaps such as empty positions, wide spans of control or missing approvers.
Legal entity Canvas
A registered company within a group, used to separate budgets and approvals.
Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) Both products
An AI method that finds relevant records first and then writes an answer based on them, with sources.
Cited answer Both products
An AI answer where each statement links to the source it came from.
Access-aware retrieval Both products
Searching only the records a person is allowed to see before the AI answers.
What-if simulation Both products
A read-only preview of how a change would affect plans, people or money.
Goal planning Both products
Comparing ways to reach a target, such as a value, margin or finish date.
Signal Both products
An early warning about a problem, such as a project slipping or an approval waiting too long.
Watch agent Both products
An AI agent that picks up a new warning and prepares its fix for approval.
Voice Ask Both products
Asking ORQYNE a question by speaking instead of typing, with a cited answer.
Draft-then-approve Both products
A way of working where AI prepares a change and a person must approve it before it happens.
Job agent Both products
An AI agent that runs a defined job, such as a status pack, and prepares drafts for approval.
Bring your own key (BYOK) Both products
Using your own AI provider key instead of the vendor's.
