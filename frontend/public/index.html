Delivery Consulting, Project Management as a Service and Delivery Governance | Orgainse Consulting

Turn Fragmented Work Into Visible, Governed Execution

Orgainse Consulting helps Information Technology services companies, software teams, startups, and growing businesses move from disconnected tools and reactive reporting to structured, accountable execution. We combine business consulting, Project Management as a Service (PMaaS), workflow transformation, and the ORQYNE delivery-governance platform to establish clearer ownership, stronger governance, reliable reporting, and better-informed decisions.

Strategy is rarely the problem; execution usually is. Work is fragmented, ownership is unclear, risks surface late, reporting is inconsistent, and leaders spend too much time reconstructing what is happening. Orgainse builds the execution system that makes priorities, responsibilities, dependencies, decisions, progress, and risks easier to see and manage. The objective is not to add another tool; it is to create greater execution certainty.

How Orgainse works

  1. Diagnose — clarify the desired outcome, current workflow, decision owners, constraints, risks, and measurable sources of leakage.
  2. Design — create the target workflow, governance model, reporting rhythm, roles, controls, and success measures.
  3. Implement — configure the process, ORQYNE workspace, templates, integrations, and operating cadence.
  4. Govern — review adoption, risks, decisions, delivery health, and measurable outcomes on a defined schedule.

ORQYNE

ORQYNE is a service-backed, multi-tenant delivery-governance platform by Orgainse Consulting. It connects project execution, portfolio visibility, governance, team knowledge, decisions, and Artificial Intelligence-assisted workflows through two purpose-built experiences: IT Services and Software, and Canvas Business Management. Artificial Intelligence assistance can be grounded in approved tenant knowledge using Retrieval-Augmented Generation, with access, model selection, and approvals governed by the customer's workspace configuration.
